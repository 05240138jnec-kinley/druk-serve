Terms and Conditions

  • About: Druk Serve helps customers in Bhutan find and book home services. The work is done by the Service Provider, not by Druk Serve.
  • Accounts: Give correct details and keep your password private. One email can have one Customer and one Service Provider account. You are responsible for your account.
  • Customers: Give a correct address, date and time, be available for the job, respect providers, and give honest reviews.
  • Service Providers: Give true details about your skills, rate and hours. Arrive on time and work safely. Mark a job Completed only when it is finished. Confirm cash only when you really received it. Providers are shown only after the Admin verifies them.
  • Bookings: A request starts as Pending. The provider accepts or declines, then marks it Completed. The price comes from the provider's hourly rate.
  • Payments: You pay after the job is done, online or in cash. Cash stays Pending until the provider confirms it. Online payments use a test gateway for now. We do not store card numbers.
  • Cancellations and refunds: A customer can cancel a Pending or Confirmed booking. There are no automatic refunds. For a problem, write to support@drukserve.bt with your booking number and we will help find a fair solution.
  • Rules: No unlawful, abusive or fraudulent use, no fake reviews, no taking bookings or payments outside the website.
  • Suspension: The Admin can suspend an account that breaks these terms.
  • Our role: We introduce customers and providers. We do not guarantee the quality or timing of any service. These terms follow the laws of Bhutan and may be updated.

Privacy Policy

  • What we collect: your name, email, phone, location and address, your password (stored only as a one-way hash), bookings, payment records, reviews, notifications, and a profile picture if you add one.
  • How we use it: to run your account, handle bookings and payments, send notifications and reset links, verify providers and keep the website safe.
  • Who sees it: for a booking, the customer and provider see each other's name and phone, and the provider sees the service address. Verified providers' profiles and reviews are public. The Admin can see data needed to run the platform.
  • No selling: we do not sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • Protection: passwords are hashed, every request is checked against your role, and reset links expire after 30 minutes. Please use a strong password and log out on shared devices.
  • Your choices: edit your details in My Profile, remove your photo, or ask us at support@drukserve.bt to correct your data or close your account.
